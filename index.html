Tips for Traveling in the Philippines with Kids

Crucial tips to make an 8-9 day island stay effortless.

Always Book Private Banca Boats

Never join large group joiner tours. A private boat costs only ~$35-$65 USD for the entire family. It lets you depart at 7:30 AM before the heat, head back whenever the child is tired, and avoids waiting for 15 strangers.

Pack Child Rashguards & Water Shoes

The midday tropical sun is strong. Long-sleeve UPF 50 rashguards eliminate sunscreen battles. Neoprene water shoes protect little feet from small coral pieces or sea grass while wading in ankle-deep sandbars.

Pick Direct Beachfront Resorts

Staying directly on the sand (e.g., South Palms on Dumaluan or Station 1 in Boracay) means one parent can easily take the kids for an afternoon nap while the other stays enjoying the beach just 20 meters away.
